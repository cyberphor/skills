# Homemade Pizza Recipe

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast (1 packet)
- 1 1/2 cups warm water (about 110°F / 43°C)
- 1 tablespoon sugar or honey
- 3 1/2 to 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons fine salt

### Sauce
- 1 can (14–15 ounces) crushed or finely diced tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil (or a few torn fresh basil leaves)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Pinch of sugar (optional, to balance acidity)

### Toppings (example)
- 2–3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- Fresh basil leaves
- Sliced pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers, or your favorite toppings
- Extra olive oil for brushing the crust

## Equipment
- Large mixing bowl
- Wooden spoon or dough hook and stand mixer
- Clean kitchen towel or plastic wrap
- Two 12-inch pizza pans or a large baking sheet
- Rolling pin or your hands for stretching the dough

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Dough
1. In a large bowl, combine the warm water, yeast, and sugar or honey. Stir gently and let it sit for 5–10 minutes until the surface looks foamy.
2. Add 2 1/2 cups of flour, the olive oil, and salt to the yeast mixture. Stir until a shaggy dough forms.
3. Gradually add more flour, a little at a time, until the dough comes together and is soft but not overly sticky.
4. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 6–8 minutes, until smooth and elastic. (If using a stand mixer, knead with a dough hook on medium-low for about 5 minutes.)
5. Lightly oil a clean bowl, place the dough inside, and turn it to coat with oil.
6. Cover the bowl with a kitchen towel or plastic wrap and let the dough rise in a warm place for 1–1 1/2 hours, or until doubled in size.

### 2. Make the Sauce
1. While the dough rises, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the minced garlic and cook for 30–60 seconds, stirring, until fragrant but not browned.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, pepper, and a pinch of sugar if using.
4. Simmer on low heat for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
5. Remove from heat and let cool slightly before using on the dough.

### 3. Preheat the Oven
1. About 20 minutes before baking, preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
2. If you have a pizza stone, place it in the oven while it preheats.

### 4. Shape the Dough
1. Punch down the risen dough to release excess air.
2. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and divide it into 2 equal pieces for two medium pizzas, or keep it whole for one large pizza.
3. Shape each piece into a ball and let rest for 10 minutes (this makes stretching easier).
4. Using your hands or a rolling pin, gently stretch or roll each dough ball into a round about 10–12 inches across.
5. Transfer the shaped dough to lightly oiled pizza pans, baking sheets, or a floured pizza peel if using a stone.

### 5. Assemble the Pizza
1. Spread a thin layer of sauce over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
2. Sprinkle an even layer of mozzarella over the sauce.
3. Add your desired toppings, being careful not to overload the pizza.
4. Lightly brush the exposed crust with olive oil for extra color and flavor.

### 6. Bake
1. Bake each pizza for 10–14 minutes, depending on your oven, until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbly with some browned spots.
2. If using a stone, slide the pizza directly onto the hot stone and bake, checking around the 8–10 minute mark.

### 7. Finish and Serve
1. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes.
2. Top with fresh basil leaves and a light drizzle of olive oil, if desired.
3. Slice and serve hot.

## Tips
- For a chewier crust, use bread flour instead of all-purpose.
- For extra flavor, refrigerate the dough in an oiled, covered container for up to 24 hours, then bring to room temperature before shaping.
- Experiment with toppings like caramelized onions, roasted vegetables, or different cheeses.
